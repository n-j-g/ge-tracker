How this was worked out
Prices
The OSRS Wiki real-time price API's hourly averages for every item since its history begins on —. The average instant-buy price is "high" and the average instant-sell price is "low". Gaps of up to — are filled from the last hour with trades.
Margin
What the outputs sell for after GE tax, minus what the inputs cost, per action. Charts and half-lives use the middle of high and low; "hours profitable" buys inputs at the higher price and sells outputs at the lower one, as if trading instantly. Blast Furnace chains include the coffer fee spread over the Wiki guide's bars per hour; tanning includes the tanner's fee.
GE tax
Applied at the rate in force each hour: none before the tax began in —, then the original rate, then the higher rate from —, per item, rounded down and capped.
Unusually wide
An hour when the margin enters its widest — compared with the previous — (using only past data), after at least — outside it. "Left" is the margin's gap above its median over the previous — at a later hour, as a share of the gap when it started, averaged over every such episode.
Returns to a level
An augmented Dickey–Fuller test on the daily margin. Most chains pass; Blast Furnace bars, ranarr unfinished potions, Saradomin brews and black d'hide bodies wander instead.
Moves first
Granger tests on daily price changes in both directions, plus how much of a move in one side the other catches up within a week. "Loosely linked" chains include the Blast Furnace bars, whose ore and bar prices tend to move in opposite directions on the same day, probably because processors buy ore and sell bars at once.
Price of XP
Gold lost per XP buying inputs and selling outputs instantly. A skill's price on a day is its cheapest listed method that day; methods differ in speed. Prayer uses bones at a gilded altar; Construction oak larders and mahogany tables; Firemaking yew, magic and redwood logs.
Left out
Burn and failure rates, travel, your levels and boosts, and how far a large order moves the price. Prices are in nominal gold, not adjusted for inflation. Past patterns can change with game updates.
