OSRS food economics: video storyboard

Draft visuals for each scene, drawn from real Grand Exchange prices (OSRS Wiki price API, hourly, March 2021 to 6 November 2026). Each frame is 16:9 for screen recording. "Play build" runs the scene's animation; every frame also rests in its finished state.

Scene 1

One straight line prices members' food

Bigger heals cost more, on a straight linePrice = 183 gp per HP − 2,793 gp

Beats: six everyday PvM foods appear one at a time; the line draws through them; then it extends left to where a food is worth 0 gp (about 15 HP) and on down to −2,793 gp at 0 HP. Line to land: "A food that heals nothing still costs you a slot. The market says a slot is worth about 2,800 gold, and a 15 HP food is worth nothing at all to a shark-eater."

Scene 2

Everything else lands off the line

Then add everything elsespecial effects  free-to-play  Sailing

Beats: add each group in turn. Free-to-play fish sit far above the line, special foods sit a little off it, and the top Sailing fish fly above it. Line to land: "Every food off the line is telling us something the simple rule leaves out."

Scene 3a

Free-to-play is its own market

Free-to-play has its own lineSlot worth ≈ 400 gp, not 2,800

Beats: zoom into the cheap end. Draw the free-to-play line through tuna, lobster and swordfish, then highlight swordfish (14 HP, 211 gp) against monkfish (16 HP, 147 gp). Line to land: "Swordfish heals less and costs more. That only makes sense because free-to-play players can't buy monkfish. Prices can only line up when buyers can switch."

Scene 3b

What the market charges for each effect

Price above or below the lineSame healing, different deal

Beats: pineapple pizza heals 22 per slot like a manta, but in two bites, and sells about 440 gp cheaper: that's the cost of the extra eat. Anglerfish's overheal costs about 300 gp more. Karambwan combo-eats yet sits 150 gp below the line, because karambwans are everywhere and the buyer at the margin doesn't need combo eating. Line to land: "Each gap is a price tag on a mechanic."

Scene 3c

Marlin took the "best food" premium

When marlin arrived, 22 HP stopped being the bestPrice above the line through monkfish, shark and sea turtle

Beats: before Sailing, 22 HP was the most healing in one slot, and those foods sold far above the line through the cheaper fish: the top step is bought by the players whose slot is worth most. Then marlin (24 HP) arrives and the dots slide left: manta, tuna potato and dark crab lose about a third of their premium. Anglerfish loses much less, because no Sailing fish overheals like it does. Line to land: "Marlin took the crown for raw healing, so only the foods selling raw healing lost their premium." Careful: anglerfish still fell a little, and Sailing changed other things too, so say "consistent with", not "proves".

Scene 3d

Sailing fish, week by week

Sailing launch to nowWeek of —
—

Beats: at launch every Sailing fish is scarce and far above the line (arrows show values off the top). Within weeks the low-level fish crash below the line: players train on krill and haddock and sell the catch regardless of price. Then they climb back toward it. Halibut and marlin stay high all year: their buyers fight where every tick of eating counts. Line to land: "New goods start mispriced, and you can watch the market find the line."

Scene 4

Bluefin and yellowfin price their extras like potions

The extra effect, priced by its potionPremium = price above the line

Beats: bluefin restores 5 prayer. A prayer potion restores 124 points at 99 Prayer for 9,261 gp, so 5 points cost 373 gp; bluefin sits 399 gp above the line. Yellowfin restores 20% run energy; the cheapest way to buy that (energy potions) costs 78 gp, and yellowfin's premium is 45 gp, nowhere near the 538 gp it would cost in super energy. The weekly line shows bluefin's premium came down to the prayer value over the year. Line to land: "Two different resources, priced against each other by players who never did the arithmetic."

Prices are average traded prices over the 8 weeks to 6 November 2026; the line is fitted on monkfish, shark, sea turtle, manta ray, dark crab and tuna potato. The weekly animation refits the line every week on those six only, so the Sailing fish are out-of-sample. Heals and effects from the OSRS Wiki.
